Rules

Gameplay Considerations

The following gameplay changes apply to both the TF2 and TF2C servers.

  1. No Random Crits
  2. No Random Bullet SpreadPellet-based weapons fire in a fixed pattern.
  3. All-TalkEach team can hear the other in voice chat.
  4. Sniper Reverse Damage FalloffSniper Rifles have reverse damage falloff applied to them, with headshots only registering as critical hits at distances greater than 1200 Hammer units.
  5. Sniper Reduced Bodyshot DamageSniper Rifles only do up to 2.5x base damage on fully-charged bodyshots.
  6. Distance-Based Jarate/Mad Milk Effect DurationThe duration of the Jarate and Mad Milk's effects are based on the coated player's distance from the center of the splash, with a minimum time of 2 seconds.
  7. Role LimitsAn experimental role limit plugin is in use. There may be up to 4 of each role. (pick, support, power)
  8. Class Limits In addition to role limits, the following class limits apply within those roles.

    Pick

    • Scout4
    • Spy2
    • Sniper1

    Support

    • Pyro4
    • Engineer2
    • Medic2

    Power

    • Soldier4
